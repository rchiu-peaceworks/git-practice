# Shortlist

15 places researched against the inclusion test in `niche.md`: located in Tokyo, single discipline, practiced under one chef/lineage for 15+ years or across generations, no menu diversification.

**A note on verification (per the task instructions):** every entry below was pulled from live web search results with a named source, not generated from memory — but I have not personally visited or called any of these, and dish/reservation details can go stale. Before this ships, spot-check at least the address/status of a few, since a place closing or details drifting is the exact kind of error this exercise is meant to catch. Task 15 (verify three places in person) is the real check.

**Narrowed to Tokyo-only (2026-08-06):** the original 16-place list spanned Tokyo, Kyoto, Osaka, and Nagoya. Since the operator lives in Tokyo and Task 15 requires in-person verification, the scope was cut to Tokyo only — dropping Honke Shibato (Osaka), Honke Owariya, Tempura Kyoboshi, Wakuden, Hirasansou (all Kyoto), and Unatou Nakagawa (Nagoya). That left 10 Tokyo places, so 5 more were researched to stay in the 15-20 range (#11-15), one of which (Edomasa, yakitori) was found to have closed its Tokyo location in 2022 and was swapped out before inclusion.
