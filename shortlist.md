# Shortlist

16 places researched against the inclusion test in `niche.md`: single discipline, practiced under one chef/lineage for 15+ years or across generations, no menu diversification.

**A note on verification (per the task instructions):** every entry below was pulled from live web search results with a named source, not generated from memory — but I have not personally visited or called any of these, and dish/reservation details can go stale. Before this ships, spot-check at least the address/status of a few, since a place closing or details drifting is the exact kind of error this exercise is meant to catch.

**Trimmed from the original 18:** dropped Tenjaku, Rengatei, Hyotei, and Kikunoi Honten — all clearly pass the craft/lineage bar but are Michelin-starred and well known enough internationally to work against the "not a tourist-list top pick" spirit of the niche. Replaced with two lower-profile, equally verifiable places (#10, #16) to stay in the 15-20 range.
